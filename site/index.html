Physical memory

Regions follow from the boot header and from where v86 puts things: setup at 0x80000, command line at 0x8e000, the 32-bit part always at 1 MiB. v86 does not expose the e820 map; SeaBIOS builds it from the RAM size, so it matches the bars below (usable RAM, with the 0x9fc00–0x100000 hole reserved).

    Each square is a 4 KiB page of guest RAM, coloured by the region the boot header implies and, when the site has a symbols.json for the kernel, by ELF section. Pale squares were all zero at the last scan; a dot marks pages that changed between the last two scans. Non-zero pages below 1 MiB include what SeaBIOS left behind and the BIOS copies at 0xc0000–0xfffff. Hover over a page to see its symbols, click it to inspect it.
